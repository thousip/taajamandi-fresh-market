Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver within our delivery area around Lingampally, Hyderabad (up to 7.4 km from our store). The areas we currently serve are listed at the bottom of our home page and at checkout. Your delivery address is checked against the delivery area when you place an order; if it is outside, the order cannot be placed.

When we deliver

  • Deliveries start on 1 December 2026. Before that date you can book an advance delivery for 1 December or later.
  • Advance delivery: choose a future delivery date at checkout.
  • Same-day delivery (from 1 December 2026): delivered the same day, usually within 2–3 hours, where offered at checkout.

The delivery date you chose is shown in your order confirmation and in My orders.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free for orders of ₹249 or more. Orders below ₹249 have a delivery charge of ₹30. The order value used is the value of the items after any offers, before any coupon. The exact charge is always shown at checkout before you place the order. Members with free deliveries left in their membership period pay no delivery charge.

At delivery

  • Our delivery partner may call the phone number in your address if they cannot find you.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, please pay the amount shown on your order to the delivery partner.
  • If a delivery cannot be completed (for example you cannot be reached), we will contact you to arrange another attempt.

Questions about a delivery: WhatsApp +91 76719 13761.
