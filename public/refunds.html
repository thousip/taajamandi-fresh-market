Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge until we start packing it. To cancel, message us on WhatsApp +91 76719 13761 with your order number. Once packing has started, the order can no longer be cancelled.

If you paid online and the order is cancelled before packing, we refund the full amount to your original payment method.

Online payments that are not completed

If you choose online payment and the payment is not completed, your order waits for payment and you can pay from My orders. An order that is still unpaid 30 minutes after it was placed is cancelled automatically and its items are returned to your basket. You are not charged.

Damaged, spoiled or missing items

If any item is damaged, spoiled or missing, tell us on the day of delivery on WhatsApp +91 76719 13761, with your order number and a photo of the item. After checking, we refund the value of those items:

  • Paid online: refunded to your original payment method.
  • Cash on Delivery: you are not charged for those items.

Orders cancelled by TaajaMandi

If we have to cancel your order (for example an item is unavailable or we cannot deliver), we tell you and refund any amount you paid online in full.

Refund timelines

Online refunds are processed through our payment partner Razorpay and normally reach your account within 3–5 working days after the refund is issued. The exact time can depend on your bank or card issuer.

Coupons

If an order with a coupon is cancelled, the coupon use is returned to you, subject to the coupon's own dates and rules.

Memberships

You can cancel a membership from the membership page at any time. Your membership stays active until the end of the current period and is not renewed after that, so there are no further charges.
