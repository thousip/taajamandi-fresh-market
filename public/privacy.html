Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information TaajaMandi collects when you use our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and/or phone number.
  • Delivery details: the addresses you save, including the location point used to check that we deliver there.
  • Your location: if you allow it, your device location is used to check whether we deliver to you. You can refuse; ordering then needs a saved address inside our delivery area.
  • Orders: the items, amounts, delivery dates, notes and status of your orders, coupons used and membership status.
  • Payments: online payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive the payment status and reference numbers; we do not receive or store your full card, UPI PIN or bank login details.
  • Basket: items you add before signing in are kept in your browser until you sign in.

How we use it

  • To take, pack and deliver your orders and to collect payment.
  • To provide refunds, coupons, memberships and customer support.
  • To keep our service secure and to keep records of orders and payments.

Who sees it

  • Delivery partners see the name, phone number and address for the deliveries assigned to them.
  • Packing staff see the items and delivery area of orders, not your name, phone number or street address.
  • Service providers that run our service for us: Razorpay (payments), Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (website hosting).

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can update your saved addresses on the website. To ask for a copy of your information or for your account to be deleted, message us on WhatsApp +91 76719 13761. Some order and payment records may need to be kept as required by law.
